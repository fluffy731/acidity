The Vibe

A quiet room that gets loud on the right nights

Acidity Bar & Coffee takes cues from Japanese renovation style — natural timber, soft greenery, low light — reworked into a space that's just as easy for a slow morning coffee as it is for a packed gig night. Come for the espresso, stay for the set.
