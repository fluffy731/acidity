Host Your Event

Private Functions & Events

Host your next celebration at Acidity with exclusive use of our entire venue. From intimate birthdays and engagement drinks to wedding celebrations, corporate functions and late-night after-parties, Acidity offers a relaxed and stylish setting with a full bar, six rotating craft beer taps, wine, sake, spirits and signature cocktails. Every function booking includes exclusive use of the venue, a dedicated bar team and a three-hour beverage package tailored to your group size.

Important information
  • All function bookings include exclusive use of the entire venue.
  • Beverage packages operate for three hours unless otherwise agreed.
  • Weekday launch rates apply to eligible Sunday-to-Thursday bookings and are subject to availability.
  • Package inclusions apply only to the selected event beverage menu.
